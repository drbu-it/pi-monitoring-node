## 1. The problem (5 min)

- Toner, paper and page counts are checked by walking to each printer.
- The Xerox sends alert emails with no control over timing. It is on or off.
- Page counts are needed for billing and for knowing which printers are worth keeping.

## 2. What we want (5 min)

- See page count, toner level and print log for every DRBU printer without being on site.
- Get a warning before toner runs out, not after.
- A monthly print report per location.

## 3. The idea (10 min)

```
printers --SNMP--> Raspberry Pi (collector) --> storage --> remote view
                                            \-> alert digest (email)
```

- The Pi sits on the DRBU network and asks each printer for its numbers on a schedule.
- SNMP is the standard way printers report counts and supply levels.
- The same Pi will later run UPS monitoring (NUT) and the camera footage transfer. Not your part, but it shapes the OS install.

## 4. Decisions - your input (25 min)

| # | Decision | Options | Notes |
|---|----------|---------|-------|
| 1 | **Where do we view it?** | Google Sheet / Grafana dashboard / email digest only | Sheet is simplest. Grafana has better charts and more upkeep. |
| 2 | **What do we track?** | Page count, toner %, paper, faults, per-user print log | Per-user logs need more than SNMP on most printers. |
| 3 | **How often do we poll?** | Hourly / daily | Counts change slowly. Toner alerts want hourly. |
| 4 | **When do we alert?** | Toner threshold, digest times | Starting point: 20% toner, digest at 8am and 5pm. |
